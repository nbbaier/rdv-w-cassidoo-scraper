## Interview question of the week

This week's question:\
**There's a ghost in your keyboard! It keeps typing weird symbols in your words. Given a string you typed (with the ghost), `#` deletes the last character and `~` duplicates it, if one exists. Return the final text.**

Examples:

```
ghostwriter("ghost")
> "ghost"

ghostwriter("a#~b")
> "b"

ghostwriter("ab~c#")
> "abb"

ghostwriter("BBB##O~")
> 'BOO'
```
