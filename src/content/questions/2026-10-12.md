## Interview question of the week

This week's question:\
**You're trick-or-treating down a street, and you're given an array that shows how many pieces of candy each house gives out. You can't visit two houses that are next to each other. Return the maximum number of candy pieces you can collect.**

Example:

```
> maxCandy([2, 7, 9, 3, 1])
> 12 // visit houses with 2, 9, and 1
> 
> maxCandy([5, 1, 1, 5])
> 10 // it's worth it to skip the middle two
> 
> maxCandy([4])
> 4
```
