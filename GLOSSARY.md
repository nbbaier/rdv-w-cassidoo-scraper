# Cassidoo Interview Questions Archive

A public, read-only web archive of the weekly interview questions from Cassidy Williams' (Cassidoo) newsletter. It exists to be the best way to browse and search those questions; it is not a practice platform, community, or product.

## Language

**Issue**:
One edition of the Cassidoo newsletter, identified by its publication date and Buttondown URL.
_Avoid_: post, article, email

**Question**:
The "interview question of the week" segment extracted from an Issue — the prompt plus its examples. Exactly one per Issue (when present).
_Avoid_: problem, challenge, exercise

**Answers-only Issue**:
An Issue containing only answers to a previous Question and no new one. No genuine example is currently known — the six Issues once believed to be answers-only all contained Questions.

**Title**:
A short human-written-quality summary of a Question, added during Enrichment (not present in the newsletter itself).

**Topic**:
A tag describing what a Question is about (e.g. strings, recursion), drawn from a controlled vocabulary and assigned during Enrichment.
_Avoid_: tag, category, label

**Number**:
A synthetic ordinal assigned by the scraper based on feed order — an internal sort key only, never shown in the UI. A Question's public identity is its date.

**Enrichment**:
The pipeline step that adds Title and Topics to a freshly scraped Question.
